EARNEST MONEY DEPOSIT (EMD)  1. The Bidder shall furnish, as part of its Bid, an Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) equivalent to an amount of 3% (Three percent) of the Estimated Project Cost. The EMD shall be refundable to unsuccessful bidder not later than 30 days from the Bid Due Date, except in the case of the successful Bidder. 2. Any Bid not accompanied by the EMD shall be summarily rejected by NMC as non- responsive.  3. The EMD of unsuccessful Bidders will be returned promptly without any interest, but not later than 30 days after the opening of financial bid, or within 15 (fifteen) days of issue of LOI / Orders to the Preferred Bidder, whichever is earlier.  4. The EMD of the successful bidder shall be returned against the signing of the agreement and submission of performance bank guarantee. 5. The EMD shall be forfeited as Damages without prejudice to any other right or remedy that may be available to NMC under the Bidding Documents and/ or under the Agreement, or otherwise, under the following conditions:
